XIAOCHEN SUN
PROJECTS ABOUT CONTACT

Xiaochen Sun is a documentary photographer based in Chengdu, China.

He graduated from Southwest Petroleum University in 2017 with a Master’s degree in Engineering. After graduating, he began working as a photographer, focusing on the lives and circumstances of individuals caught in the profound social changes of contemporary China.

His work has been published in publications including China National Geography, Sanlian Life Week, Caixin, and Window of the South, as well as in the photography sections of various online media outlets.

In 2020, he was nominated for the World Press Photo Joop Swart Masterclass. In 2023, his photobook BOSTAN was selected for the 23rd Pingyao International Photography Festival and exhibited there. In 2025, he was shortlisted for the “Journalists’ Home” Award for Reporting on Major Social Events.
